# Capture-ready scout demonstration

Run the ordinary Compatibility project. On Mars, choose Curiosity and DRIVE LOCAL TERRAIN. Wait for READY and visual refinement. F9 opens presets; choose PREPARE REPEATABLE SCOUT SCENARIO and wait for READY. It may prepare a different sourced Gale slope patch; if no suitable measured scenario passes validation, the prepared rock scenario is visibly SIMULATED OBSTACLE. Do not begin recording during preparation or replanning.

## Six shots for 1:45–2:45

1. **Overhead map, 1:45–1:53.** Press 1: orthographic registered imagery. Keep the line visibly unverified. Voiceover: “Our answer is not another map to stare at.”
2. **Map entry, 1:53–1:57.** Press 2: four-second camera descent within the prepared local scene. “It is a map you can enter.” For globe-to-local capture, use an edit across the actual preparation handoff with matched destination framing. Do not describe this as one uninterrupted world.
3. **Progressive terrain, 1:57–2:08.** Press 3: low-to-high visual refinement of measured elevation, then surface styling. Denser interpolation is not newly observed resolution. “NASA mission data shapes a Martian environment we can explore.”
4. **Data transformations, 2:08–2:24.** Press 4: elevation → registered imagery → derived slope → science destinations. Each transformation holds three seconds. Capture extra holds if required by editing. “Elevation shapes the world. Orbital imagery reveals the surface. Derived slope informs proposed routes. Scientific observations become destinations.” Science labels represent archived rover localizations associated with Rocknest and John Klein; exact sample offsets are unknown.
5. **Provenance, 2:24–2:31.** Press 5. The card names products, source/prepared spacing, coverage, dates, documented accuracy and source links. “Each layer remains traceable to its source, sampling and coverage.” F8 reduces other HUD elements; keep the card legible.
6. **Scout-to-human, 2:31–2:45.** Press 6: HUD retracts, original activation cue plays, assessment map stays visible. Drive forward until the actual 10 m scout scan detects the hazard, stop, wait for the revised proposal, then H to walk as the astronaut. Findings originate from proximity/visibility, not the recording clock. If NO ROUTE FOUND appears, reset or choose another safe goal; do not conceal failure as a successful plan.

Shortened closing narration for the tight timing:
- 2:31–2:39: “Curiosity becomes our forward scout. The player inspects Mars through the rover before stepping onto the terrain.”
- 2:39–2:45: “It detects hazards, updates the map and proposes a new course for the astronaut.”

The final loop takes more than six seconds to perform live on the available hardware. Record it separately and edit the genuine discovery, completed map update and handoff into the allocated window. Raw footage, editing and voiceover are not generated by this implementation.

## Repeatability and honest labels

F10 resets staging positions, findings, unverified proposal and camera framing without rebuilding terrain. Rover pace restores after H returns from 1× human control. Shift-click sets a different proposal and invalidates older worker results. Escape returns to the globe with session findings. Returning to the same prepared region reuses its cache.

Record at 1920×1080, 1600×900 or 1366×768. Camera, HUD and provenance presets adapt to these sizes. Prefer Compatibility and performance graphics if the measured frame times require it. Capture stable shots after worker preparation; optional Forward+ volumetric lighting is substantially slower on this laptop. Disable F9's panel with F8 for the clean take. Keep essential simulation/source labels when presenting a hazard or claimed scientific target. SOURCE APPEARANCE removes cosmetic tint, microdetail, gravel and tracks while preserving measured geometry, lighting and atmospheric approximation.

Movement, weather, collision rocks, human route costs, proposed routes and new scout detections are illustrative gameplay. They are not historical NASA traverse data, certified traversability analysis or reconstructed weather. The original mobility field lab remains a separate direct-load scene.
